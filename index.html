The Human Authority Treaty · H.A.T.

One Humanity. Sovereign Nations.Shared Authority.

In Part III of HUMAN INTELLIGENCE: The Final Say (p. 38), the Quorum + One proposes a global Human Authority Treaty (H.A.T.), inspired by the Patent Cooperation Treaty (PCT), administered by the World Intellectual Property Organization (WIPO).

For decades, the PCT has demonstrated that nations can cooperate through a common international framework while retaining their sovereign authority over decisions within their borders.

The Human Authority Treaty proposes applying that proven principle to the Autonomous Era.

Under this proposed framework, participating nations would establish shared minimum requirements for autonomous systems while preserving national sovereignty over implementation, enforcement, and authorization within their jurisdictions.

Common standards for identity, accountability, verifiable authority, and cross-border recognition could enable autonomous systems to operate internationally without granting them unrestricted freedom to act.

Rather than requiring the world to construct an entirely new model of international cooperation, H.A.T. draws inspiration from an established institutional approach—offering a practical starting point for negotiations in an era when technological progress is moving faster than governance.

Every nation, small or large, deserves a voice in humanity's FINAL SAY.

Let markets reveal the best autonomous systems over time, but let humanity establish the governance foundation beneath them together.

The future may be autonomous. Authority must remain accountable to humanity.

The H.A.T. Treaty is a proposal presented in the manifesto, not an existing international agreement or an initiative administered or endorsed by WIPO.
