Official Rules

BWRCI Limited Edition Drawing

Connected with The AI Commandments challenge at thefinalsay.org/commandments.

NO PURCHASE OR PAYMENT OF ANY KIND IS NECESSARY TO ENTER OR WIN. A PURCHASE WILL NOT INCREASE YOUR CHANCES OF WINNING. VOID WHERE PROHIBITED.

1. Sponsor

The drawing is sponsored and administered by Better World Regulatory Coalition Inc. ("BWRCI" or "Sponsor"), Chicago, Illinois. BWRCI acts as curator of entries submitted through thefinalsay.org.

2. Eligibility

Open only to legal residents of the fifty (50) United States and the District of Columbia who are 18 years of age or older at the time of entry. Employees and officers of the Sponsor, the author of HUMAN INTELLIGENCE: The Final Say, and the members of their immediate families and households are not eligible. Subject to all applicable federal, state, and local laws and regulations.

3. Entry period

The drawing begins on October 29, 2026 and ends at 11:59 p.m. Central Time on January 10, 2027 (the "Entry Period"). Sponsor's computer is the official clock for the drawing.

4. How to enter

During the Entry Period, visit thefinalsay.org/commandments, submit your answers to the challenge (or choose to view the answers), and complete the entry form with a valid email address, confirming your eligibility and agreement to these Official Rules.

  • Limit one (1) entry per person and per email address. Additional entries will be disregarded.
  • Your score in the challenge has no effect on your eligibility or your chances of winning. A score of 0 of 7 and a score of 7 of 7 have identical odds.
  • Entries that are incomplete, automated, or submitted by any means other than the entry form are void.

5. Prizes

Twenty-five (25) prizes will be awarded. Each prize is one (1) numbered, full-color Limited Edition hardcover copy of HUMAN INTELLIGENCE: The Final Say. Approximate retail value: US $50 each. Total approximate retail value of all prizes: US $1,250. Prizes are shipped free of charge to an address within the United States. No cash or other substitution, except that Sponsor may substitute a prize of equal or greater value if a prize becomes unavailable. Prizes are not transferable.

6. Drawing and odds

On or about January 15, 2027, Sponsor will select the winners in a random drawing from among all eligible entries received during the Entry Period. Odds of winning depend on the number of eligible entries received.

7. Winner notification

Potential winners will be notified by email at the address provided on their entry. A potential winner must reply within fourteen (14) days with a U.S. shipping address and confirmation of eligibility. If a potential winner does not reply in time, is found ineligible, or a notification is returned as undeliverable, that prize may be forfeited and an alternate winner selected at random from the remaining eligible entries.

8. Your information

Entries are collected through Formspree on behalf of BWRCI and used to administer this drawing. If you check the updates box, BWRCI may also send you occasional updates about the book and related work; you can unsubscribe at any time. If you check the feature box, BWRCI may publish your reasoning on thefinalsay.org with your first name and city. BWRCI does not sell entrant information.

Separately from the entry form, the challenge page may record anonymous answer data (with no email or name) to show combined results such as how many readers identified each voice.
