In summary
What is most striking about these seven responses is not that they agree on every rule. They do not. What is striking is that they independently converge on a recognizable ethical baseline. HUMAN INTELLIGENCE: The Final Say · p. 82

Truthfulness, humility, protection of human agency, refusal of domination, care for the vulnerable. The machines can name the baseline. Only humans can decide to hold them to it.
